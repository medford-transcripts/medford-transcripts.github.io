Help verify the record

These transcripts are produced by machine, and the machine is wrong in specific, findable ways. This page lists where a person’s judgment would help most. You do not need to be technical, and you do not need to fix anything — telling us that a line is already right is as useful as telling us it is wrong.

How to submit

On any transcript page, right-click a line and a form opens with that line pre-filled. Edit it and submit, or submit it unchanged to confirm it is correct. Every accepted change becomes a public commit.

The form has to be opened from a transcript line — that is what attaches the meeting, the timestamp and the current text to your submission. Opening it on its own cannot carry any of that.

A mistyped date in a video title moves a meeting to the wrong day and stops it matching its agenda. Eleven were corrected in September 2026 using four checks — a missing agenda, the wrong weekday, who was present, and the resolution numbers cited. These two resist all four.

  • City Council — titled 1/7/25

  • City Council — titled 08/01/25

    A Friday, and this council has met on a Tuesday 92–100% of the time every year since 2011. Also only 195 seconds long.

What the archive cannot tell you

Some gaps are not transcription errors and cannot be fixed by reprocessing: a microphone that was off, a vote taken by voice with no names spoken, a meeting nobody recorded. If you know how a particular vote went — from the posted minutes, or from being in the room — that is information the recording does not contain.
