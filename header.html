This is our own search index, not Google's. Google and Bing have indexed only part of this archive and we cannot see or control which part, so this searches an index we build ourselves and it runs entirely in your browser — nothing you type here is sent anywhere. It covers meeting titles, bodies and dates, the officials, staff and regular participants who spoke, and the AI summaries of the meetings that have one. It does not yet search the full text of the transcripts; for that, open a meeting and use your browser's find.
