# Human meeting recaps

Recaps written by sitting Medford officials and posted to r/medfordma. They
are the best available specification for what a useful meeting summary looks
like, written by people who were in the room.

| file | author | meeting |
|---|---|---|
| `2026-09-14_school-committee_erika.md` | u/Erika02155 | School Committee 9/14/26 |
| `2024-12-17_city-council_kit.md` | u/kitschc | City Council 12/17/24 |

## What to copy

- **Agenda order.** Both follow the order items were taken up. Kit says so
  explicitly: "go in the order of the printed agenda".
- **Outcome first.** "We approved X." "We tabled the petition because the
  petitioner was not present." "The Council rejected an amended motion to..."
  The decision leads; the discussion follows.
- **Timestamps into the recording** for anything a reader might want to hear
  themselves ("Councilors' comments start around 3:39").
- **Plain outcomes without tallies.** Neither recap lists who voted which way.
  That happens to match what this pipeline can support: roll-call votes are
  short turns, where speaker attribution measured 53-66% against a human
  reference versus 97% on substantive speech.

## What NOT to copy

Both authors are elected members writing in the first person, and both add
things that are not in the transcript:

- **Opinion.** "This is a big deal!" / "[I think this is great...]". They are
  entitled to a view. An archive is not.
- **Outside knowledge.** Kit explains that the CDB must hold a public hearing
  before the Council votes, and that Tufts is exempted by the Dover Amendment.
  True, useful, and not said in the meeting -- so a generated summary must not
  produce it, because there is no way for a reader to check it against the
  source we publish beside it.
- **Personal framing.** "I make no claim of these notes being comprehensive."
  Ours carries an AI-generated banner instead.

The generated summary should read like the skeleton of these: same structure,
same outcome-first phrasing, none of the voice.

## Length

| | words |
|---|---|
| Erika, School Committee 9/14/26 | ~900 |
| Kit, City Council 12/17/24 | ~1,400 |
| Kit, three meetings 11/19-12/3 | ~2,000 |
| **target for generated** | **250-400** |

These are written to be read *instead of* watching a five-hour meeting. Ours
sits directly above the full transcript, so it does not have to carry the
whole meeting -- it has to get a reader to the part they care about. One
sentence per item, decision first.
