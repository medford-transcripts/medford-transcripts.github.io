The Medford Historical Commission and Medford Historic District Commission are civic bodies responsible for reviewing demolition applications, designating historically significant structures, managing local historic districts, and advancing preservation and archaeological initiatives across Medford. Meeting records held by the archive run from February 2024 through October 2026. ongoing

Thomas Brooks Park master planning and rehabilitation

ongoing

The commission partnered with consultants to design and execute a multi-phase master plan for Thomas Brooks Park. Early phases focused on fieldstone wall maintenance, restoring Pomp's Wall, and completing preliminary archaeological investigations. Phase Two and Three plans advanced through Community Preservation Act funding, incorporating ADA-compliant history walks, interpretive signage, subtle stone markings of the former Brooks estate house, lilac plantings, and tree maintenance. Phase Two archaeology contractor SWCA was selected in early 2025, and tree clearing alongside ground-penetrating radar preparations continued through mid-2026.
