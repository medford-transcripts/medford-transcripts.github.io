Demolition delay administration and ordinance revisions

ongoing

The commission routinely evaluates demolition applications for historic properties, imposing 18-month delays or approving early releases following negotiated architectural plan revisions [M01#2, M03#1, M06#8]. To address procedural ambiguity when properties are sold during delay periods, the commission sought legal advice and met with the Building Commissioner to discuss enforcement and ordinance updates [M07#5, M10#3, M26#6]. Members explored streamlining initiatives, such as considering automatic significance for pre-1900 buildings and establishing fast-track procedures for minor outbuildings [M31#7, M32#5, M59#6]. Recently, the commission created subcommittees to update delay directives and coordinate historical protections with citywide zoning incentives [M58#8, M67#1, M77#7].

Cemetery assessment and restoration

ongoing

The commission established ongoing preservation initiatives for Medford's burial grounds, obtaining Community Preservation Act funds for stone wall maintenance and headstone repairs [M32#7, M39#5]. Exploratory trenching at the former Cross Street Cemetery site revealed intact burials and human remains, prompting state coordination and extended archaeological assessments [M42#6, M57#6]. The commission funded preliminary fieldwork for headstone foundations, initiated pilot restoration programs for veteran gravestones, and commissioned ground-penetrating radar testing [M44#3, M58#7, M69#6, M74#12]. Restoration projects and grant applications for Cross Street Cemetery and Oak Grove Cemetery remained active into late 2026 [M74#12, M77#7].
