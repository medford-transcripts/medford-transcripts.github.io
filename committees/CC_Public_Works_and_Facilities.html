The City Council Public Works and Facilities Committee oversees Medford's physical infrastructure, including road and sidewalk maintenance, municipal building repairs, and large-scale transportation projects. During the 2024-2025 term, the committee focused on improving pavement management by transitioning from external contractors to in-house repair crews. It also acts as a forum for reviewing state-led projects, such as MassDOT intersection redesigns and highway noise mitigation. As of June 2025, the committee is working on a comprehensive inventory and maintenance assessment of all city-owned property.

Pavement and Sidewalk Management

ongoing

The committee began the term by requesting detailed reports on past spending and the feasibility of establishing an in-house crew for road repairs. DPW officials reported that $8.5 million in annual funding is required to maintain city standards for streets and sidewalks. Following the passage of a local override, the committee reviewed plans to allocate $500,000 for equipment and three new highway division staff to perform spot repairs internally.
