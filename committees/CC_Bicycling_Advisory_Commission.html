The CC Bicycling Advisory Commission is a municipal body in Medford that advises on cycling infrastructure, safety policy, and community outreach. Meeting since April 2022, the commission has worked to expand the city's bike network, manage the local Bluebikes system, and distribute safety equipment to residents. Its work spans major state-led redesigns of rotaries and the development of regional connectors like the Clippership Connector. The body also advocates for legislative changes and improved road safety enforcement through collaboration with the police department. As of late 2026, the commission is focused on filling membership vacancies, implementing contraflow lane projects, and standardizing its strategic goals.

Bicycle Infrastructure and Lane Projects

ongoing

Early work focused on connecting Medford Square to the Green Line Extension via bike lanes on Main and George Streets. The commission later evaluated complex designs for Salem and Winthrop Streets, focusing on bus stop interfaces and parking impacts. Most recently, the body has overseen the painting of a contraflow lane on Park Street and identified potential connections on Fulton Street.

Safety Advocacy and Law Enforcement Coordination

ongoing

Following several fatal crashes and a member's injury, the commission began sending formal letters to MassDOT and the DCR requesting safety reviews of state-owned roads.
