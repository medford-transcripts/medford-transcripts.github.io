The Medford Zoning Board of Appeals is a regulatory municipal body responsible for adjudicating petitions for dimensional and use variances, special permits, comprehensive permits under M.G.L. Chapter 40B, and administrative appeals from decisions of the Building Commissioner. The board evaluates proposals for compliance with local zoning requirements, reviews architectural, civil engineering, and traffic peer analyses, and attaches conditions and mitigations to development approvals. The archive holds 46 meetings of the board dating from May 15, 2023, through September 24, 2026, spanning three municipal terms. Over this timeframe, the board resolved several major multifamily 40B applications and heard dozens of residential and commercial requests for alterations, additions, and change-of-use relief. At the most recent meeting, the board approved residential and commercial use petitions while continuing hearings on multiple accessory dwelling unit applications to review modified designs and consult legal counsel on state regulations.

settled

The applicant submitted alternative site plans reducing building massing along Amaranth Avenue, introducing five-story step-downs, and adjusting site circulation to reduce traffic on Myrtle Street. The board conducted focused hearings on traffic studies, intersection safety, stormwater systems, EV charging stations, and architectural facades. In May 2024, the board determined the market-rate units were subject to Medford's linkage ordinance and voted to approve the comprehensive permit with an $816,564.24 partial linkage fee waiver for public improvements. In January 2025, the board approved an associated special permit allowing partial demolition of an existing commercial building on the property to accommodate a lot split.
