The CC Zoning Board of Appeals (ZBA) adjudicates petitions for variances, special permits, and findings under the Medford zoning ordinance. Since early 2023, the board has managed several large-scale Chapter 40B comprehensive permit developments, requiring extensive peer reviews of traffic, civil engineering, and architectural massing. Its routine business includes resolving residential disputes over fences, setbacks, and multi-family conversions. At its most recent sessions, the board has focused on interpreting new state regulations regarding accessory dwelling units and the conversion of non-conforming commercial spaces into residential or service-based uses.
