The Medford Zoning Board of Appeals adjudicates petitions for variances, special permits, and Chapter 40B comprehensive permits for residential and commercial developments. Meeting from late 2023 through late 2026, the board has managed significant growth in high-density housing while addressing individual property hardships such as flooding and topographical constraints. Its work frequently involves reconciling large-scale multifamily projects with neighborhood concerns regarding traffic, massing, and infrastructure. As of September 2026, the board is focused on interpreting updated state guidelines regarding accessory dwelling units and the conversion of non-conforming commercial spaces into residential uses.
