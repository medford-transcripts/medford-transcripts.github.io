The Medford Conservation Commission is responsible for protecting the city's natural resources through the enforcement of the Wetlands Protection Act and the review of development projects impacting resource areas. Its work from early 2023 through late 2026 includes permitting for regional dam improvements, managing invasive species in the Middlesex Fells, and overseeing the expansion of public waterfront access. The commission issues negative determinations of applicability and orders of conditions to ensure that construction projects include native plantings, erosion controls, and stormwater mitigation. At its most recent meeting, the commission was focused on finalizing certificates of compliance for state-managed projects and preparing for a formal review of new local wetlands regulations.
