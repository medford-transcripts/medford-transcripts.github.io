Spanning from May 2022 through May 2026, the committee conducted public hearings, addressed fixed cost increases, and weighed staffing and academic investments against available municipal revenues. Over successive cycles, the committee submitted municipal requests rising from $72.4 million for FY23 to $87.58 million for FY26. Most recently, the committee approved the preliminary FY27 operating budget, which accommodates a $4.1 million base increase alongside $3.3 million in net reductions.
