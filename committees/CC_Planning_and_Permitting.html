The Planning and Permitting Committee is a committee of the Medford City Council responsible for reviewing and drafting municipal land use regulations, zoning code overhauls, development standards, and property ordinances. Its work covers reviewing comprehensive zoning updates, corridor master plans, environmental performance standards, and housing regulations in coordination with city planning staff and external consultants. The archive covers 36 meetings of the committee held between February 28, 2024, and May 5, 2026. Over this span, the committee has systematically processed citywide zoning changes, including specialized districts for major transit corridors and commercial squares, environmental green scoring, and state-mandated accessory dwelling unit provisions. At its most recent meeting, the committee advanced a draft rezoning proposal for the Boston Avenue corridor and Tufts Institutional District for a joint session with the Community Development Board.

settled

The committee designated the Mystic Avenue commercial corridor as an urgent rezoning priority to replace auto-oriented parcels with walkable, multi-story mixed-use development (M08#5, M11#1). ongoing

In January 2025, consultants presented a zoning proposal to replace single-family districts with tiered neighborhood residential zones allowing accessory units and historic conversions (M19#1). The committee approved motions to scale down proposed density tiers across several neighborhoods and requested improved public outreach (M19#2, M20#2). Accessory Dwelling Unit regulations

settled

The committee initially addressed accessory dwellings through definition updates for manufactured and tiny homes during the Phase 1 zoning review (M06#6). Following state legislative changes, the committee reviewed local regulations to govern accessory dwelling units in residential districts, exploring special permits and short-term rental bans (M21#3, M23#2, M24#2). The committee evaluated draft rules allowing ADUs while setting local special permit standards for second units on single lots and conversions of historic accessory structures (M26#3). The committee voted to refer the ADU zoning regulations favorably to the City Council for referral to the Community Development Board (M26#4).
