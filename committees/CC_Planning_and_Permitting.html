The City Council Planning and Permitting Committee is a standing committee of the Medford City Council responsible for evaluating and drafting municipal zoning updates, land-use policies, and property-related ordinances. Its work centers on modernizing the city's zoning code to encourage walkable mixed-use growth, increase housing capacity, establish environmental performance standards, and regulate vacant and converted properties. Across twenty-six meetings from August 2024 through May 2026, the committee reviewed comprehensive district-by-district rezoning proposals developed alongside municipal staff and planning consultants. At its most recent meeting in May 2026, the committee referred an amended zoning proposal for the Boston Avenue corridor to the full City Council for a joint hearing with the Community Development Board while continuing discussions on institutional zoning for Tufts University.
