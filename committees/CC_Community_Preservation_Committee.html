The Community Preservation Committee (CPC) manages Medford's Community Preservation Act (CPA) funds, allocating resources toward open space, historic preservation, and affordable housing. Meeting regularly since at least July 2024, the committee evaluates applications from both municipal departments and private community organizations through an annual funding cycle. Major projects supported include park renovations, the restoration of historic municipal and religious buildings, and seed funding for the city's Affordable Housing Trust. The committee also maintains a small grant program for lower-cost projects and handles emergency restoration requests for historic structures. As of October 2026, the body is reviewing a new slate of fiscal year 2027 applications and exploring a long-term funding strategy for the Medford High School construction project.
