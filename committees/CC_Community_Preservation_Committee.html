The Medford Community Preservation Committee evaluates applications and recommends allocations of Community Preservation Act funds across affordable housing, historic preservation, and open space and recreation projects. The committee has been meeting regularly across the 2022-2023, 2024-2025, and 2026-2027 terms to review grant submissions, manage annual budgets, and update local application policies. Over this span, it has recommended funding for municipal building rehabilitations, public park and school athletic improvements, cemetery preservation, and housing initiatives. As of October 2026, the committee is reviewing initial applications for the fiscal year 2027 funding round, including proposals for rental assistance, City Hall exterior repairs, athletic court renovations, and tree plantings.
