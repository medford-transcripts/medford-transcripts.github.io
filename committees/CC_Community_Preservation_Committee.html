The archived record spans meetings from December 2022 through October 2026. Historic church and non-profit facility preservation

ongoing

The committee routinely reviews and recommends CPA grants for historically significant non-profit and religious structures, incorporating strict preservation conditions and restrictions. Initial funding supported restoration at Shiloh Baptist Church and the Isaac Hall House [M02#2, M02#5]. The committee instituted policies referring off-cycle requests to standard annual cycles unless emergency thresholds were met [M11#1]. Subsequent major recommendations included siding restoration for the Unitarian Universalist Church, emergency masonry work at the Royall House, and multi-phase stained glass window restoration at Shiloh Baptist Church [M14#6, M19#2, M21#5].
