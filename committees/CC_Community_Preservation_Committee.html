The CC Community Preservation Committee administers local Community Preservation Act funds derived from property tax surcharges and state trust fund matches to support affordable housing, open space, recreation, and historic preservation projects in Medford. The committee's archived meetings cover a timeline extending from March 12, 2018, through November 10, 2026. Over this span, the body has evaluated annual grant applications, established funding set-asides, monitored active capital projects, and executed emergency and small grant recommendations. At its November 10, 2026 meeting, the committee evaluated FY27 proposals for shoreline design and Affordable Housing Trust support, approved an emergency roof grant for the Unitarian Universalist Church, and reallocated lighting funds between municipal parks.

Affordable housing and Walkling Court redevelopment

ongoing

The committee evaluated affordable housing initiatives early in its tenure, reviewing proposals from local housing organizations for multi-unit construction and heating conversions (M02#3, M03#2, M03#3). In late 2022, the Medford Housing Authority presented a multi-million dollar proposal for the expansion and redevelopment of Walkling Court (M11#7). By mid-2026, construction for Walkling Court Phase 1 was confirmed to begin in late summer 2026 (M33#4).

ongoing

During the same period, the committee recommended window restoration funds for the historic Isaac Hall House (M11#5, M13#5). Shiloh Baptist Church later secured additional recommendations for ADA accessibility improvements and Phase 2 stained-glass restoration across subsequent grant cycles (M25#9, M29#5, M35#5). In late 2026, the committee evaluated further requests for Shiloh Baptist Church windows and voted to approve an emergency roof grant for the Unitarian Universalist Church (M37#1, M38#3).

Condon Shell facility and lighting upgrades

ongoing

Initial CPA support for the Condon Shell was introduced in 2018 to fund exterior cleaning, masonry weatherproofing, and lighting repair requests (M03#1). In 2023, the committee approved a $3,276 small grant to install electrical outlets and safety lighting for public performances and events (M17#2). Proponents returned in late 2024 with a request for app-controlled field and walkway lighting, which received a recommendation for $98,285 in funding (M24#1, M25#4). In late 2026, to address unexpected construction cost shortfalls, the committee approved reallocating $50,000 from an existing Tufts Park grant to complete the Condon Shell lighting work (M36#4, M38#4).

Small Grants Program policy and expansion

settled

The committee created a Small Grants Program in 2018 to offer a streamlined application pathway for funding requests up to $5,000 (M01#3). The program was sustained with annual reserve set-asides for rolling grant awards supporting park tree inventories and school play equipment (M06#2, M19#2, M20#2). In early 2025, the committee formally adopted policy changes that doubled the maximum small grant award limit to $10,000 and removed mandatory matching requirements (M26#4).
