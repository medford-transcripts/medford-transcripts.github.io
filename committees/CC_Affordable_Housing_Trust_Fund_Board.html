The CC Affordable Housing Trust Fund Board serves as a municipal bank and policy body dedicated to creating and preserving affordable housing in Medford. Established in August 2024, the board oversees the distribution of housing funds, evaluates city-owned land for development, and implements strategic housing plans. The body has spent its initial years establishing governance structures, securing Community Preservation Act funding, and drafting a five-year Action Plan. Most recently, the board has been finalizing standardized developer application templates, preparing for its first housing resource fair, and advancing ordinance language for housing linkage fees.
