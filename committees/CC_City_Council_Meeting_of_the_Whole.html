The Medford City Council Committee of the Whole serves as a legislative and deliberative body of all council members to review policy proposals, conduct departmental budget hearings, evaluate consultant requests, and examine municipal ordinances before final council votes. The committee has met consistently across multiple council terms from January 2023 through October 2026. Its recurring business centers on municipal fiscal oversight, Community Preservation Act allocations, zoning overhauls, charter and administrative reorganizations, and local regulatory ordinances. Most recently, the committee advanced a procurement request for proposals for the city's next multi-year phase of zoning updates and held an executive session on municipal litigation.
