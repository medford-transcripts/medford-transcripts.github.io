The Medford City Council Committee of the Whole serves as a committee of all council members convened to conduct detailed reviews of municipal ordinances, annual departmental budgets, capital authorizations, grant allocations, and legal matters. Its meetings span from October 2023 through October 2026 across three legislative terms. The committee regularly interrogates department heads and outside consultants on city operations, drafting and amending local legislation before reporting matters to the regular City Council meeting. At its most recent meetings, the committee has been engaged in multi-year comprehensive zoning consultant procurement, residential property tax exemption analysis, public tree regulations, and executive sessions regarding pending municipal litigation.

Deliberations continued through June 2026 with formal presentations from the Mayor, police, public works, and school administration on the FY27 budget.

ongoing

In September 2026, the committee authorized planning staff to issue a Request for Proposals to select a consultant for the next multi-year phase of the zoning updates project.

Leaf blower regulations

settled

The committee reviewed proposed Paper 21-057 to regulate leaf blowers, hearing testimony from city officials, residents, and commercial landscapers. In November 2023, the committee approved eleven policy amendments standardizing operational hours with the noise ordinance, allowing year-round electric blower use, establishing commercial fees, and instituting seasonal gas blower restrictions. In February 2024, the committee adopted additional text amendments regarding phased gas equipment transitions and voted to report the draft ordinance out of committee. Departmental preparations and code enforcement responsibilities for the ordinance were later reviewed during subsequent Building Department budget hearings.
