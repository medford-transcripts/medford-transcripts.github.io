The Community Development Board serves as Medford's planning board, responsible for reviewing site plans, issuing special permits, administering subdivision controls and Approval Not Required lot endorsements, and formulating zoning recommendations for the City Council. The archive documents 76 meetings held between May 2023 and October 2026. Across this period, the board's business shifted from reviewing discrete commercial and institutional developments toward undertaking an extensive citywide zoning overhaul covering commercial corridors, neighborhood residential districts, and mixed-use squares. The board also conducts annual public hearings on Community Development Block Grant expenditures and priorities. As of October 2026, the board approved an amended commercial site plan and reviewed draft citywide zoning cleanup amendments covering height definitions, formatting, and development incentives.
