MBTA Communities Act multifamily overlay compliance

settled

Pursuant to state requirements under MGL Chapter 40A Section 3A, the board guided the drafting of a transit-oriented multifamily overlay district. Deliberations centered on parcel capacity around the Wellington Orange Line station, base building heights, and reduced parking ratios. In December 2023, the board recommended that the City Council adopt the Wellington Station Multifamily Overlay District with parking set between 0.5 and 1.2 spaces per unit. Minor corrective text amendments addressing setback standards, green requirements, and building coverage definitions were recommended to the City Council in March 2025.
