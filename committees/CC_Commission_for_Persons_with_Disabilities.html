The CC Commission for Persons with Disabilities advocates for the equal status of residents with disabilities by monitoring ADA compliance in Medford’s municipal infrastructure, programs, and services. Since 2023, the body has focused on removing physical barriers at public schools, City Hall, and transit stations while organizing annual community resource fairs. The commission maintains a digital repository of accessibility resources and provides input on inclusive designs for city playgrounds and polling places. As of late 2026, the body is collaborating with the Institute for Human Centered Design on a city-wide accessibility transition plan and prioritizing intersections for the installation of accessible pedestrian signals.
