The Commission for Persons with Disabilities advocates for the equal status of disabled residents in Medford's political and social spheres. The body advises on city infrastructure projects, monitors compliance with the Americans with Disabilities Act, and coordinates public resources. Meeting records from 2024 through 2026 show a focus on improving physical accessibility at City Hall, transit stations, and polling locations. As of the most recent meeting, the commission is prioritizing city intersections for accessible pedestrian signal upgrades and reviewing city-wide transition plans.
