Annual license renewals and population quotas

ongoing

At the close of each year, the commission reviews and approves annual renewal lists for all retail all-alcohol and wine and malt licenses in the city. The board uses these renewal periods to condition approvals on explanations for long-term closures and track businesses that cease operations or decline renewal. The commission also certifies annual estimated resident population figures for submission to the ABCC to establish statutory license quotas. During renewals, commissioners coordinate with municipal departments to verify that licensees hold valid common victualler permits.
