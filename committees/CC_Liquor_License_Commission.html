The Medford Liquor License Commission regulates the sale, service, and licensing of alcoholic beverages in the City of Medford. The commission oversees permanent and temporary liquor licenses, approves operational and structural modifications, reviews police incident reports, and conducts disciplinary hearings for license violations. The archive covers seven meetings held between October 23, 2024, and September 16, 2026. At its most recent meeting, the commission approved multiple temporary licenses and permanent license amendments, and took under advisement a disciplinary hearing concerning alcohol service to a minor.

Disciplinary hearings and compliance enforcement

ongoing

The commission conducts disciplinary proceedings and reviews incident reports concerning alleged liquor law violations and underage alcohol sales. In November 2024, following police testimony on underage sales, the commission ordered a 10-day license suspension and mandatory training for Medford Wine and Spirits, while clearing Razzo's Bar & Grill of violations following a separate police report review. In January 2025, the commission held a hearing on four allegations of underage alcohol sales at Main Street Liquors and took the matter under advisement. Most recently, the commission took under advisement allegations against the Great American Beer Hall regarding alcohol service and consumption by a 19-year-old minor.
