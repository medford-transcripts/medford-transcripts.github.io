The MPS High School Building Committee oversees the planning, design, and eventual construction of a modernized high school in partnership with the Massachusetts School Building Authority (MSBA). Formed in early 2024, the committee has navigated the eligibility and feasibility phases, including the hiring of project managers, architects, and a construction manager. As of late 2026, the body has narrowed 29 design alternatives down to a single preferred addition-and-renovation schematic. The project is currently preparing for a city-wide debt exclusion vote scheduled for June 2027.

Site Constraints and Deed Restrictions

ongoing

Site analysis revealed significant challenges including an 80-foot grade change, lack of fire protection systems, and a 1967 deed restriction. The discovery of these restrictions forced the committee to eliminate certain design options that encroached on land owned by the Department of Conservation and Recreation (DCR). The committee continues to negotiate with state agencies regarding emergency egress and the potential use of fire roads on DCR land. Current site plans utilize a teardrop loop to manage bus and special education traffic while improving overall safety.

Financial Planning and Budgetary Efficiencies

ongoing

Initial cost estimates for the various design options ranged between $850 million and $935 million, prompting the committee to seek significant efficiencies. Through a series of votes in 2026, the body approved reductions in classroom sizes, auditorium seating, and parking program targets, saving an estimated $150 million. The committee also eliminated a proposed 'learn to swim' basin and field-over-parking structures to control costs. As of November 2026, the total project cost is estimated at under $700 million, with a substantial portion anticipated for MSBA reimbursement.
