The MPS High School Building Committee convenes to manage and execute the Massachusetts School Building Authority (MSBA) building project for Medford High School. Commencing its inaugural meeting in May 2024, the committee guided the project through early eligibility requirements, project management procurement, design team selection, and feasibility studies. Across its meeting history, the committee systematically evaluated dozens of building options, space efficiency proposals, site layouts, and mechanical systems. Following the selection of a preferred schematic design, the committee finalized space reductions and selected a Construction Manager at Risk to advance the project toward its next design phase. At its most recent meeting in October 2026, the committee approved contract negotiations with Suffolk as Construction Manager at Risk, adopted mechanical and solar recommendations, and established a revised total project cost estimate of approximately $699 million.

settled

Initial design kickoff targeted MSBA reimbursement bonuses by adhering to specialized stretch codes and LEED standards [M16#5, M19#2, M24#6]. Environmental modeling included a 50-year Life Cycle Cost Analysis comparing geothermal ground-source, air-to-water heat pump, and hybrid HVAC systems [M49#6, M56#6].
