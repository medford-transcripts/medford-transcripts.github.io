The MPS Strategic & Capital Planning Subcommittee oversees the district's long-term facility improvements and multi-year organizational goals. The body manages the prioritization of capital projects, coordinates with state funding programs like the Massachusetts School Building Authority, and addresses operational challenges such as enrollment shifts and space constraints. Since late 2024, the subcommittee has been developing a comprehensive five-year strategic plan informed by community engagement. As of the most recent records, the body is focusing on integrating facility planning with academic grade configurations and finalizing its strategic vision for 2027.
