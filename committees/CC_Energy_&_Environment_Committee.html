The CC Energy & Environment Committee is an advisory public body in Medford dedicated to environmental policy, municipal sustainability, waste reduction, climate adaptation, and community outreach. Meeting over 35 sessions from June 2022 through October 2026, the committee has reviewed municipal building decarbonization, draft tree ordinances, solid waste contracts, urban ecology, and regional climate legislation. Major lines of work include endorsing local tree ordinances, supporting the launch of the Electrify Medford heat pump coaching campaign, expanding annual Harvest Your Energy Festival programming, and tracking citywide Climate Action Plan implementation. Tree Ordinances and Canopy Protection

ongoing

ongoing
